\section[Assessment]{\textbf{ONERA M6 Overall Assessment}}

% ============================================================
% ONERA M6 -- Overall Assessment
% ============================================================

\begin{frame}{\small ONERA M6 -- Overall Assessment}
\scriptsize

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Aerodynamics}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Limited grid and code-to-code variability is observed overall, except for $C_D$, which exhibits substantially larger differences.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Droplet Impingement}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Good overall agreement is observed, although impingement extent remains more sensitive to spatial and droplet-distribution resolution than integrated water mass.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Convective Heat Transfer \& Thermodynamics}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Despite close agreement in $T_{\mathrm{rec}}$, substantial code-to-code differences emerge in $HTC$, convective heat transfer, and particularly freezing fraction.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{block}{\scriptsize Ice Accretion}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Comparable water impingement does not translate into comparable ice accretion, with substantial variability in mass partition and final ice geometry.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{0.10cm}

\begin{alertblock}{\scriptsize Overall Takeaway}
Code-to-code variability increases through the icing simulation chain, from comparatively consistent aerodynamics and impingement to substantially greater differences in thermodynamics and final ice accretion.
\end{alertblock}

\end{frame}
